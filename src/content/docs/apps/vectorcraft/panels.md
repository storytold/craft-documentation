This page lists every panel, library and bar that the **Window** menu opens, with what each one does, its default shortcut and the guide that covers it.

Choose a panel in the **Window** menu to show it, and choose it again to hide it. The menu lists the panels in alphabetical order in your interface language. To find a panel by name, choose **Help → Search Commands…** (**Ctrl+Shift+/**, or **Cmd+Shift+/** on macOS) and type part of its name. A panel with no default shortcut can be given one in **Edit → Keyboard Shortcuts…**, where the panels are listed under Window on the **Menu Commands** tab.

![The Window menu open over a city skyline at dusk, with Help Bar, Contextual Task Bar and Tools checked, the panels listed alphabetically with their shortcuts, and the pointer on Layers, which is checked and shows F7.](/assets/uploads/vectorcraft/panels-window-menu.webp)

Press Tab to hide the panels, the Tools panel and the Control bar, and press Tab again to show them. Press Shift+Tab to hide or show only the panels.

In the Essentials workspace, the dock at the right edge of the window holds the Properties, Layers and Libraries panels as tabs. The column of icons beside it holds the other panels in groups:

- Color and Color Guide
- Swatches, Brushes, Symbols and Pattern Options
- Stroke, Gradient and Transparency
- Appearance and Graphic Styles
- Artboards
- Transform, Align and Pathfinder
- Character, Paragraph and Glyphs
- History, Actions, Info and Navigator
- Variables

When you choose a panel in the **Window** menu that sits in the icon column, its group opens as a flyout panel beside the column. Choose the panel again to put the flyout away. The » button next to the flyout's ≡ button also collapses it back to icons.

![The Essentials dock with the Layers panel in front, listing the Type, Harbor and Background layers of a harbor scene, and the Swatches panel open as a flyout from the icon column with its Brushes, Symbols and Pattern Options tabs beside it.](/assets/uploads/vectorcraft/panels-dock-groups.webp)

| Appearance | Lists the fills, strokes and effects of the selected object in stacking order, and the object's opacity. | Shift+F6 | Shift+F6 | [Apply effects and appearance](/apps/vectorcraft/apply-effects-and-appearance/) |

## Type panels

These panels are in the **Window → Type** submenu.

| Panel | What it does | Windows and Linux | macOS | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Character | Sets the font family and style, size, leading, kerning, tracking, scale, baseline shift, rotation and language of the selected type, with the All Caps, Small Caps, Superscript, Subscript, Underline and Strikethrough buttons. | Ctrl+T | Cmd+T | [Add and style type](/apps/vectorcraft/add-and-style-type/) |
| Character Styles | Lists the document's character styles. A click applies a style to the selected text, and Alt-click (Option-click on macOS) applies it and clears overrides. | None | None | [Work with styles and long text](/apps/vectorcraft/work-with-styles-and-long-text/) |
| Glyphs | Shows every character of the current font. A double-click inserts the character at the Type tool's insertion point. | None | None | [Work with styles and long text](/apps/vectorcraft/work-with-styles-and-long-text/) |
| OpenType | Turns on the OpenType features of the selected text, such as ligatures, alternates, swashes, ordinals, fractions and figure styles. | Ctrl+Alt+Shift+T | Cmd+Option+Shift+T | [Work with styles and long text](/apps/vectorcraft/work-with-styles-and-long-text/) |
| Paragraph | Sets alignment, indents, space before and after, bullets and numbering, hyphenation and the composer. | Ctrl+Alt+T | Cmd+Option+T | [Add and style type](/apps/vectorcraft/add-and-style-type/) |
| Paragraph Styles | Lists the document's paragraph styles. A click applies a style to the selected paragraphs, and Alt-click (Option-click on macOS) applies it and clears overrides. | None | None | [Work with styles and long text](/apps/vectorcraft/work-with-styles-and-long-text/) |
| Tabs | Sets tab stops on a ruler, with each stop's alignment, position and leader. | Ctrl+Shift+T | Cmd+Shift+T | [Work with styles and long text](/apps/vectorcraft/work-with-styles-and-long-text/) |

## Libraries

The four library submenus at the end of the **Window** menu open libraries of brushes, graphic styles, swatches and symbols. Each library opens in a panel of its own, and you add its items to the document from there.

| Submenu | What it lists | Guide |
|---|---|---|
| **Window → Brush Libraries** | The built-in brush libraries, **User Defined**, **Other Library…** and **Save Brushes…** | [Paint with brushes](/apps/vectorcraft/paint-with-brushes/) |
| **Window → Graphic Style Libraries** | The built-in graphic style libraries, **User Defined**, **Other Library…** and **Save Graphic Style Library…** | [Apply effects and appearance](/apps/vectorcraft/apply-effects-and-appearance/) |
| **Window → Swatch Libraries** | **Default Swatches**, the built-in swatch libraries, the **Gradients** and **Patterns** submenus, **User Defined**, **Other Library…** and **Save Swatch Library…** | [Fill, stroke and color](/apps/vectorcraft/fill-stroke-and-color/) |
| **Window → Symbol Libraries** | The built-in symbol libraries, **User Defined**, **Other Library…** and **Save Symbols…** | [Organize with layers and symbols](/apps/vectorcraft/organize-with-layers-and-symbols/) |

**User Defined** lists up to 10 libraries that you saved. **Other Library…** opens a library file or a document that you choose. **Save Brushes…**, **Save Graphic Style Library…**, **Save Swatch Library…** and **Save Symbols…** save the document's brushes, graphic styles, swatches or symbols as a library.

## Bars

These items are at the top of the **Window** menu, under **Workspace**. None of them has a default shortcut. Each workspace keeps whether each bar shows. The [Customize the workspace](/apps/vectorcraft/customize-the-workspace/) guide covers them.

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Window → Control** | Shows or hides the Control bar, which runs across the top of the window with the options of the current tool or selection. Its panel menu docks it to the top or the bottom of the window and turns each group of its options on or off. The Essentials workspace hides it. |
| **Window → Help Bar** | Shows or hides the strip at the bottom of the window that lists the current tool's hints, separately from the status bar. It shows by default. |
| **Window → Contextual Task Bar** | Shows or hides the bar that floats under the selection with buttons for common commands on it, such as Group, Duplicate and Lock. Its **More Options** menu has **Hide Bar**, **Pin Bar Position**, **Reset Bar Position** and **Show Properties Panel**. |
| **Window → Tools** | Shows or hides the Tools panel. |
| **Window → Toolbars** | Holds **Advanced**, which switches the Tools panel between its Basic and Advanced layouts, **Double Column**, **Keep Tools Beside the Canvas**, a list of the toolbars you made, **New Toolbar…** and **Manage Toolbars…**. |

**Keep Tools Beside the Canvas** docks the Tools panel between the left-side panels and the canvas instead of at the window's left edge. It is off by default.

## Panel menus

Click the ≡ button at the right end of a panel's tab strip to open its panel menu. Every panel menu ends with **Float Panel**, which moves the panel out of the dock into a window of its own. In a floating panel, the item reads **Dock Panel** and puts the panel back in the dock. Items that cannot run on the current document, selection or panel rows are dimmed.

A few items have conditions:

- **Merge Selected** and **Reverse Order** in the Layers panel menu need two or more highlighted rows.
- **Delete Artboards** needs more than one artboard in the document.
- **Import Data Sets…** reads a `.csv`, `.tsv` or `.txt` file, and **Export Data Sets…** writes a `.csv` file. **Load Variable Library…** reads an `.xml` file, and **Save Variable Library…** needs at least one text variable.
- **Open Swatch Library** and **Open Graphic Style Library** are submenus of libraries. Choosing a library opens it in a panel of its own, as the submenus in [Libraries](#libraries) do.
